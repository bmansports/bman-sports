## Yes, I dropped two undefeated teams... 11 spots

USC is 4-0 and BYU is 3-0 and fell 11 spots each. The easier of the two to explain is USC. What do you mean you gave up 35 points to RUTGERS of all teams. A Rutgers team that lost to Boston College and UMASS OF ALL TEAMS. The Lincoln Riley defense curse is striking once again and the Trojans haven't played any team with a pulse. 

BYU is a tad more complex but what happens when Bear is forced to throw more than 25 times a game. Not every team is going to let LJ Martin run all over them. Can the Cougars actually show they can compete against actually skilled teams.

## Ole Miss vs LSU flipped the SEC

Ole Miss up 10 and LSU down 11. This was the game I had circled as the week's best, and the action and drama delivered tenfold. Lane Kiffin's squad going down flips a lot of the SEC on its head. Ole Miss looks like a complete squad that can truly test anyone in the SEC and maybe the country. Ole Miss still has a gauntlet still to come with UF next week and Missouri, Texas, Auburn, UGA, and Miss St still to come. LSU has an even tougher schedule with Bama, A&M, and Tennessee coming down the pipe. With Leavitt's struggles in Oxford, I worry if that they can survive a full SEC schedule.

## Ohio State > Texas??

So, the big shocker here likely comes from me dropping Texas to 3rd. But here me out, Texas looked very human against a very underperforming UTSA squad.

## Have yourself a week ACC!

All the big dawgs in the ACC moved up this week. The top 2 of Miami and VT are creeping up week by week landing at 7 and 14 respectively. But the big stories are the big upgrades for Duke and Louisville after huge wins over Stanford and SMU respectively. This top 4 will likely see each other duel it out to see who'll represent the ACC in the CFP this December.

## What I'm watching next week

While the ACC seems to be sorting itself out, this week will likely sort out a lot of the confusion in the SEC as 10 of the top 12 SEC teams are facing each other this week. I'm keeping an eye namely of that night cap Missouri vs Mississippi State game and Texas A&M vs LSU as the losers will likely find themselves tumble out of the uber-competitive SEC.
